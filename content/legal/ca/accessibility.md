Ayvant IT & Cybersecurity wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use **ayvant.ca**. We support the goals of the *Accessible British Columbia Act* and the *Accessible Canada Act*: identifying, removing and preventing barriers.

## Our standard

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. Among other things, that means:

- Pages that work with a keyboard and with screen readers
- Text with enough contrast to read, and layouts that hold up when you zoom to 200%
- Descriptive text for meaningful images, and headings that reflect the page's structure
- Forms with visible labels
- Pages that work on phones and tablets as well as desktop computers

## Known limitations

- **Embedded forms and chat.** Our contact form, newsletter sign-up, scan request forms and chat are provided by HighLevel (LeadConnector). We can't fully control their accessibility. If you have any trouble with them, call us at **(604) 239-2090** or email **hello@ayvant.ca** and we'll help you directly.
- **Email security scanner.** The scanner on our Free Email Security Scan page is an embedded tool. If it doesn't work for you, email us your domain and we'll run the scan and send you the results.
- **Older articles.** Some blog articles may have images without full descriptions. We're working through them.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something on the Site doesn't work for you, please tell us. Let us know the page and what happened, and how you'd like us to get back to you.

- Mail: Ayvant IT & Cybersecurity Inc., 20627 Fraser Hwy, Langley, BC V3A 4G4

We aim to respond within 2 business days and to provide the information or service you need in another way while we fix the problem.

## Ongoing work

We test the Site with automated tools and by hand when we make changes, and we review this statement at least once a year.
