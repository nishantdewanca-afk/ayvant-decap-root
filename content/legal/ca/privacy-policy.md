This Privacy Policy explains how **Ayvant IT & Cybersecurity Inc.** ("Ayvant", "we", "us") collects, uses and discloses personal information when you visit **ayvant.ca** (the "Site"), fill in a form, chat with us, subscribe to our newsletter, take our security check or otherwise contact us.

We are based in Langley, British Columbia. Our handling of personal information is governed by British Columbia's **Personal Information Protection Act** ("PIPA") and, where information crosses provincial or national borders in the course of commercial activity, by Canada's **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act** ("PIPEDA").

**What this policy does not cover.** When we provide IT support, cybersecurity or consulting services to a client, we handle that client's data under our written agreement with them, and we act on our client's instructions. Our client's own privacy policy applies to that information. Please don't send us client files, patient records or other confidential information through the Site's forms or chat.

## Quick summary

- We collect what you give us (such as your name, email, phone number and message) and some technical information about your visit.
- We use it to reply to you, provide and improve our services, send the newsletter you asked for, and keep the Site secure.
- Analytics cookies, and advertising cookies if we add them, run **only if you opt in**. You can change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer.
- We don't sell personal information.
- Some of the companies that help us run the Site store information in the **United States**.
- You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Contact our Privacy Officer at [hello@ayvant.ca](mailto:hello@ayvant.ca).

## 1. Personal information we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Contact and assessment requests:** name, email, phone number, company, the type of firm you work in, and your message.
- **Newsletter sign-up:** your email address and name if you give it.
- **Dark web scan and email security scan requests:** your name, email address and company domain, so we can run the scan and send you the results.
- **Chat:** what you type into the chat window, and the contact details you share there.
- **Security check:** when you finish the check, your answers, your score and the type of firm you selected are sent to us without your name or contact details, so we can see which risks are most common. They're stored with Netlify.
- **Calls and emails:** what you tell us when you call or write to us.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Technical information:** IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, the page that sent you to us, and the date and time of your visit. Our hosting provider keeps this in server logs for security.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see section 5 and our [Cookie Policy](/pages/cookie-policy/).

**Information from others**

- Business contact information that is publicly available, or that someone shares with us when they refer you.

## 2. Why we collect it and how we use it

We collect and use personal information only for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate, and we tell you those purposes when we collect it. We use it to:

- Reply to your questions, quote, and arrange assessments and consultations
- Provide, support and bill for our services if you become a client
- Send the newsletter and updates you asked for (see section 8)
- Run the scans and security check you request and send you the results
- Understand how the Site is used and improve it (only with your consent to analytics cookies)
- Show you our ads on other sites and measure them (only if we add advertising cookies and you consent to them)
- Keep the Site, our systems and our clients secure, and prevent fraud and spam
- Meet our legal and regulatory obligations, and establish or defend legal claims

If we want to use your information for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first unless the law permits or requires the new use without it.

## 3. Consent

By giving us your information through a form, chat, email or call, you consent to our using it for the purpose you gave it for. For optional cookies, we ask for your **express (opt-in) consent** through the cookie banner.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, unsubscribing from emails, or changing your **Cookie settings**, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. We will explain what withdrawing may mean for you, for example that we may not be able to reply to a request or continue a service.

## 4. Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with other companies for their own marketing. We disclose it only:

- **To service providers** who help us run the Site and our business, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only for our purposes. They include:
  - **Netlify** (website hosting and security check results) — United States
  - **HighLevel / LeadConnector** (contact, newsletter and scan request forms, chat and email) — United States
  - **Google** (Google Analytics, only with your consent; Google Maps on our About and Contact pages) — United States
  - **GitHub** (stores the Site's content) — United States
  - Our email, accounting and scanning tools, where needed to provide what you asked for
- **To Meta Platforms**, only if we add the Meta Pixel to the Site and you opt in to advertising cookies (see our [Cookie Policy](/pages/cookie-policy/))
- **When the law requires or permits it,** for example to respond to a court order or a lawful request from authorities, to investigate fraud or a security incident, or to protect someone's safety
- **If our business is sold or reorganized,** to the parties involved, under confidentiality terms, as PIPA permits

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies to run the Site. **Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) load only if you accept them.** We don't currently use advertising cookies. If we add the Meta Pixel to show you our ads on Facebook and Instagram, it will load only if you opt in to advertising cookies, and we will update this policy and our Cookie Policy before we do.

If your browser sends a **Global Privacy Control** signal, we treat it as a refusal of advertising cookies. You can change your choices at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer. Details are in our [Cookie Policy](/pages/cookie-policy/).

## 6. Storage and processing outside Canada

Several of our service providers are based in the **United States** or store information there, as listed in section 4. While your information is in another country, it is subject to that country's laws and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities. We choose providers with strong security practices and require them by contract to protect your information. If you have questions about our providers outside Canada, contact our Privacy Officer.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires:

- **Enquiries and form submissions:** up to 2 years after our last contact, unless you become a client
- **Client records:** for the term of our agreement and 7 years after, for tax, accounting and legal purposes
- **Newsletter:** until you unsubscribe, then we keep a record of the unsubscribe so we don't email you again
- **Security check results:** up to 2 years
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months
- **Server logs:** up to 90 days

If we use your personal information to make a decision that directly affects you, we keep it for at least one year after that decision, as PIPA requires, so you have a reasonable opportunity to ask for access to it. When we no longer need information, we destroy it or make it anonymous.

## 8. Email marketing

We send commercial emails such as our newsletter only with your consent, as Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (**CASL**) requires. Every email identifies us, includes our mailing address, and has an **unsubscribe** link. We process unsubscribe requests promptly, and within 10 business days at the latest. You can also unsubscribe by emailing [hello@ayvant.ca](mailto:hello@ayvant.ca).

## 9. How we protect it

We protect personal information with security arrangements that suit its sensitivity, including encryption in transit, access controls and multi-factor authentication, limiting access to people who need it, staff confidentiality obligations, and contracts with our service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

**Privacy breaches.** If a breach of security safeguards involving personal information under our control creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and report it to the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** as PIPEDA requires, and we will keep a record of every breach. Where appropriate, we will also notify the **Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia**.

## 10. Your rights

Under PIPA and PIPEDA you can:

- **Ask for access** to the personal information we hold about you, how it has been used, and to whom it has been disclosed
- **Ask us to correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete
- **Withdraw your consent**, as described in section 3
- **Ask questions or make a complaint** about how we handle your personal information

To make a request, write to our Privacy Officer at [hello@ayvant.ca](mailto:hello@ayvant.ca) or the address in section 13. We may need to confirm your identity before we respond. We will respond within **30 business days** unless the law allows us more time, in which case we will tell you why. We don't charge for corrections. We may charge a minimal fee for access to your personal information, and if we do, we will give you a written estimate first. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why, as the law allows, and how to ask for a review.

**If you're not satisfied** with our response, you can contact the **Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia** ([oipc.bc.ca](https://www.oipc.bc.ca)), or, for matters under PIPEDA, the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** ([priv.gc.ca](https://www.priv.gc.ca)).

## 11. Children

The Site is for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 19 through it. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and if a change is significant we will tell you more prominently, for example with a notice on the Site or by email.
