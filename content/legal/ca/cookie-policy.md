This Cookie Policy explains how **Ayvant IT & Cybersecurity Inc.** ("Ayvant", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on **ayvant.ca** (the "Site"). It forms part of our [Privacy Policy](/pages/privacy-policy/).

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include pixels (tiny pieces of code that send information to another company's servers) and local storage. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

**First-party** cookies are set by our Site. **Third-party** cookies are set by other companies whose services run on our Site, such as Google.

## Your choices

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept optional cookies. **Nothing optional runs until you say yes.** You can:

- **Accept all**, **Reject non-essential** or **Choose** which categories to allow
- Change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer of every page
- Turn on **Global Privacy Control** in your browser. We treat it as a refusal of advertising cookies

If you turn a category off after accepting it, we stop loading those cookies and delete the ones we set on our own domain. Cookies stored by other companies on their own domains can only be removed in your browser settings or with that company.

We ask again after 12 months, or sooner if we add new kinds of cookies.

## Cookies we use

### Strictly necessary (always on)

These make the Site work and remember your privacy choices. They can't be turned off in our settings, but you can block them in your browser, and some parts of the Site may then not work.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ayvant_consent | Ayvant | Remembers your cookie choices | 12 months |
| Form and chat cookies | HighLevel (LeadConnector) | Make our contact form, newsletter sign-up, scan request forms and chat work, and protect them from spam. Set only when those embedded tools load | Session to 12 months |
| Session storage | Ayvant | Remembers that we've already shown you the dark web scan offer during this visit | Until you close the tab |

### Analytics (only with your consent)

These help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it. They count visits and traffic sources and show which pages are used most.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _ga | Google Analytics | Tells visitors apart | 2 years |
| _ga_&lt;ID&gt; | Google Analytics | Keeps the state of your visit | 2 years |

Google processes this data in the United States. Its use of the data is described in [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can also install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Advertising (not in use)

We don't currently use advertising cookies. If we add the **Meta Pixel** to show Ayvant ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who have visited the Site, it will load **only if you opt in** to advertising cookies, it will not load for browsers that send a Global Privacy Control signal, and we will update this policy and ask for your consent before it runs.

### Embedded content from other companies

Some pages show content hosted elsewhere. When it loads, that company receives your IP address and browser information and may set its own cookies:

- **Google Maps** on our About and Contact pages, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **HighLevel (LeadConnector)** for our forms and chat, as described above
- **Ayvant Radar** (radar.ayvant.com), our email security scanner on the Free Email Security Scan page

## Browser controls

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies, and many can block third-party cookies by default. If you block all cookies, parts of the Site, such as our forms and chat, may not work.

## Do Not Track

There is no common standard for "Do Not Track" signals, so the Site does not respond to them. We do honor Global Privacy Control, as described above.

## Changes

We'll update this policy when we change the cookies we use, and change the "Last updated" date. If we add a new category of cookies, we'll ask for your consent again.
